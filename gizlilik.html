Tazyik Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Tazyik does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The game does not use an internet connection.
  • Your progress (stars, coins, purchased gear) is kept only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.
